# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT A STUDY VENTURE

This fictional document was written solely to demonstrate the input format.
It is not one of the 244 evaluated business plans and contains no study data.

## Business idea

Example Circular Packaging Venture proposes reusable transport containers for
local food distributors. The business would rent containers and operate a
return and cleaning service. Its intended environmental benefit is to reduce
single-use packaging waste. No measured impact results are available.

## Customers and operations

The first customers would be small distributors in one city. Revenue would
come from rental fees. The team plans to test return rates and cleaning costs
before expanding. Transport and cleaning could create additional environmental
burdens that must be measured alongside avoided packaging waste.

## Team and sustainability planning

The fictional team has logistics and product-design experience. It intends to
record container reuse cycles, packaging avoided, water consumption, transport
distance, and worker conditions. It has not yet set validated impact targets.

## Uncertainty

The venture has no prototype, customer contracts, verified lifecycle study,
financial forecast, or independently assessed sustainability outcomes. Missing
evidence should remain missing in any illustrative assessment.
